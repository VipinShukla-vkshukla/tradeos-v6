How the AI Brain Works — Simple View
👁️
It watches the market every evening
At 6 PM, the pipeline fetches fresh data — Chartink scanner results, NSE bhavcopy prices, FII flows, corporate events. All of it lands in Supabase. You don't touch anything.
🧠
It reasons over what it sees
Your rule engine (CTL + SBS + TPO) scores every stock. Then DeepSeek or Claude reads the same data and adds a conviction layer — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW — with reasons. Past lessons from your own trades feed into this reasoning.
📈
It learns from every trade you close
Every closed trade auto-generates a lesson — what worked, what failed, what rule to apply next time. These lessons feed back into AI prompts. The ML model trains on real outcomes every Sunday. It gets smarter over time.
