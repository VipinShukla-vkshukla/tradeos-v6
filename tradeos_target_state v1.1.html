Self-Evolution — Three Learning Loops Running Continuously
Weekly ML Training LoopPhase 1 · every Sunday
1
Collect Training Data
Pulls all closed_positions + linked signal_log rows. Features: RSI, vol_ratio, industry_rank, sector_rank, lifecycle, ai_conviction, fii_flag at entry time.
2
Label Outcomes
Win = pnl_pct > 0. Loss = pnl_pct < 0. Recent trades weighted 2x. 80/20 train/test split. Uses your 54 closed trades to start.
3
Train RandomForest
scikit-learn. Outputs feature importance ranking. Saves model to models/ml_model.pkl. Logs accuracy + top features to ml_training_log.
4
Update Signal Scoring
ML conviction supplements AI provider. If ML accuracy > current provider accuracy, flags switch for review. Auto-switches if provider fails.
5
Sunday Telegram Report
"ML retrained. 54→67 trades. Accuracy: 62%. Top feature: industry_rank (0.18). Drift: vol_ratio declining importance over last 30 trades."
Strategy Evolution LoopPhase 4 · every Sunday
1
Analyze 90-Day Performance
evolution_tracker.py segments closed trades by strategy (CTL/SBS/TPO), sector, lifecycle state, regime at entry time.
2
Identify Parameter Drift
CTL min_monthly_rsi=58: wins cluster at 62+? SBS vol_ratio=1.3: best wins at 1.8+? Flags statistical deviations with evidence count.
3
Write Proposal to Supabase
"CTL min_monthly_rsi: 58→62. Evidence: 78% of wins had RSI>62. Expected WR +4.2%. Based on 34 trades." Status = PENDING.
4
Your Approval Required
Telegram with [APPROVE] [REJECT] [BACKTEST FIRST]. Status stays PENDING until you act. Never auto-applies anything.
5
Apply, Track, Revert If Needed
If approved: updates strategy_config JSONB. Tracks applied_at. Compares next 90d vs baseline. Auto-proposes revert if WR drops.
Trade Lesson LoopPhase 1+ · every closed trade
1
Trade Closes
Position → closed_positions. Triggers post_trade_analysis.py. Fetches original signal_log row (entry score, RSI, vol_ratio, regime, industry_rank at entry).
2
AI Retrospective
AI receives full entry context + what happened during hold + exit reason + final P&L. Asked: what did the signal miss? What was the real driver?
3
Structured Lesson Written
Writes to lessons table: scenario_type, root_cause, corrective_rule, source=AI. Linked to original signal_log row for full traceability.
4
Feed Forward Into Future Signals
Lessons included in AI context for future signals of same scenario type. "RBLBANK-type: banking SBS in RISK OFF → check fii_flag=CAUTION first."
5
Pattern Clustering at 20+ Lessons
Groups similar root_causes. 5+ sharing "sector rotation missed" → proposes new sector momentum input via discovery_proposals table.
