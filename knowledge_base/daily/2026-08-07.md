# 3. Pattern Discovery

**Market Regime.** Still NEUTRAL (score 56, same as 6 Aug), VIX 12.16
(marginally higher, still calm), but breadth cooled: advance/decline ratio
0.75 (was 1.39) — more decliners than advancers today, a quieter market
than Friday's setup suggested going in. Nifty 20-day change actually
improved (+3.16% vs +0.93% on 6 Aug) — a market drifting up on thinning
participation, not broadening out.

**Allocator behaviour — the dominant pattern of the day.** Unlike 6 Aug,
where slot scarcity was real (2 positions actually held the slots GABRIEL
was waiting on), today's "slots spent" declines describe a capacity that
was never occupied. 6 Aug validated "the allocator enforces slot scarcity
correctly." Today complicates that: the mechanism is either re-litigating
its own ranking every cycle without ever committing to a winner, or the
recorded `TAKE` verdicts in `allocation_decisions` don't correspond to
what the live veto actually saw. Both explanations point at the same
place — the gap between what gets written to the audit table and what
actually gates an order — and neither is "the market had no opportunity."

**Institutional/execution risk.** No IP-allowlist rejections today (zero
occurrences, vs 2 blocked live orders on 6 Aug) — the Oracle server's
outbound IP held. But this isn't confirmation the fix landed; there was no
live swing exit today to even attempt an order through, so the hypothesis
from 6 Aug is untested, not resolved.

**Timing / SDN maturity.** SDN produced 3 of today's 5 closed trades (was
1-for-2 legs on 6 Aug, now adds 1 win + 2 losses) — the engine is getting
real repetitions quickly (own family, all three short-side conditions
merged, by design, for exactly this reason). Still far short of a usable
sample.

**Data integrity, not a trading pattern but bears on how much to trust
future pattern-reads.** `intraday_setups` misattributed today's TAKEN
trades to VWR when `closed_positions` says SDN/ORB — the same class of
issue (a symbol/strategy re-triggering multiple times in a session,
collapsing under the standard dedup into the wrong instance) that also
explained the SAPPHIRE anomaly on 6 Aug. Worth a standing note: any
`intraday_setups`-only analysis of "what got taken" needs `closed_positions`
cross-checked before being trusted.
