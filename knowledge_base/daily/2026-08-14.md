# 2. Decision Audit — leading with the promotion-bet resolution

**The 07-Aug MOM/RVS promotion got its first three resolved outcomes
today.** Pre-promotion baseline: MOM 50% win / +0.05% avg return
(barely positive), RVS 42% / −0.49% (negative).

**❌→✅ MANAPPURAM/MOM — a loss, but the exit mechanism worked; the
*execution* is the actual finding.** Peaked +0.43R (+2.56%), stalled,
reversed to −0.75R over 10 sessions. The stall-exit guard correctly fired
at 09:00:41 IST — 14 minutes *before* market open — diagnosing exactly
this pattern ("10 sessions... best +0.43R, now −0.69R... capital and slot
worth more elsewhere"). The automated SELL then retried **56 times**
against `MARKET_CLOSED` until the market actually opened, finally closing
at 09:16:08 at −0.75R — roughly 0.06R worse than the −0.69R the guard saw
at the moment it decided to act. **This is a different, milder failure
mode than the IP-allowlist issue** (self-resolves the moment the market
opens, no manual intervention needed, small not large slippage) but it is
a real one: a pre-market evaluation cycle produced a valid exit decision
the system then had to sit on for 15 minutes before it could act on it.

**✅ Correct Acceptance — PPLPHARMA (MOM+SEC) and AIIL (MOM), both
winners.** PPLPHARMA +0.863R over 14 sessions (peaked 12.49%, still
banked 8.05%); AIIL +0.377R over 2 sessions. Both `BROKER_EXIT` —
consistent with the give-back guard or a manual close rather than a
target hit; not investigated further today given the volume of other
findings.

**Reading the three together:** 2 wins, 1 loss (67%) — better than MOM's
50% pre-promotion win rate, on a sample of exactly three. This is
*encouraging*, explicitly not *confirming* — n=3 is far short of anything
actionable, and the one loss came with its own real (if minor) execution
finding attached rather than being a clean read on the strategy alone.

# 6. Final Verdict

**Did TradeOS make good trading decisions today?** Yes, and today is the
most substantively *tested* session of the seven reviewed — real
resolution on the MOM bet, SDN's sample nearly doubling, the give-back
guard proven on shorts, and one small, well-understood execution finding.

**Biggest mistake:** Not a decision — the 15-minute pre-market execution
delay on MANAPPURAM's stall exit, which turned a correctly-timed −0.69R
diagnosis into a −0.75R actual result. Small, self-resolving, but real.

**Best decision:** The stall exit on MANAPPURAM, independent of the
execution delay — the guard correctly recognized a position that had
gone from a real gain to a real loss over 10 sessions and called it,
which is exactly the discipline this mechanism was built for, on the
first MOM position it ever needed to apply that discipline to.

**If only ONE improvement could be made before tomorrow's session:**
Look at whether pre-market-triggered exits (today's MANAPPURAM pattern)
are a recurring shape or a one-off — if several sessions show the same
"correct call, 15-minute forced wait" pattern, that's worth a real fix;
today alone is exactly one data point, not evidence of a pattern.
