# 6. Final Verdict

**Did TradeOS make good trading decisions today?** Yes, and today reads
differently from the last two — less like a single anomaly to explain
(6 Aug's IP block, 7 Aug's zero-entry allocator puzzle) and more like the
system's designed mechanisms — give-back, stall exit, slot-driven
entry/exit turnover, automated order execution — all doing what they were
built to do, in the same session.

**Biggest mistake:** None rising to the level of the last two sessions'
findings. DEVYANI's 99-second, −0.81R loss is a plan-sized loss on a
correctly-cut invalidated setup, not a decision failure — though the gap
between `CLAUDE.md`'s account of it and the persisted allocator row is
worth closing before trusting either fully.

**Best decision:** The ETERNAL/CIPLA/SCI/AUBANK/VIJAYA sequence inside one
20-second window — two disciplined exits (give-back, stall) freeing
capacity that immediately went to two fresh, qualifying candidates. This
is the whole capital-allocation design working end-to-end, not one good
trade.

**If only ONE improvement could be made before tomorrow's session:**
Close the DEVYANI numeric gap (Item 2, Section 4) — not because it changed
today's outcome, but because this review depends on `allocation_decisions`
being a trustworthy record of what the live system actually did, and two
sessions now (7 Aug, 10 Aug) have found reasons to doubt that.
